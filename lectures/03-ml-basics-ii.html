03: Machine Learning Basics II

Monday, 27 April 2026, Hörsaal 9.122

Overview

Continuation of Lecture 02. How to evaluate a model (accuracy, precision, recall, F1) and why accuracy alone misleads on imbalanced data. Train, dev, and test splits. The perceptron algorithm as a first learning algorithm: weights, the update rule, linear separability, and the hyperparameters that matter in practice.

Topics

  • Performance measures: accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score
  • Class imbalance and why accuracy can be misleading
  • Training, development, and test splits (60/20/20)
  • Training error vs. test error, overfitting, regularization
  • The perceptron algorithm: artificial neurons, weights, and the update rule
  • Dot products and prediction scores
  • Linear separability
  • Hyperparameters: feature selection, number of iterations, early stopping, step size
