01: Introduction

Monday, 13 April 2026, Hörsaal 9.122

Overview

Course structure, expectations, and a first look at the questions the course addresses: what AI means in its current form, how modern AI systems compare to human intelligence, and why language sits at the centre. Half-length session, oriented around motivation rather than content.

Topics

  • Course logistics: 12 lectures, 3 seminar weekends, 1 exam
  • Artificial Intelligence vs. Human Intelligence
  • AI in the current context: AlphaFold, agentic AI, multimodal data
  • Key questions: What are neural networks, language models, transformers?
  • Where will AI go next?
  • Where does machine thinking come from?
