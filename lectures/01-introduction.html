Monday, 13 April 2026 — Hörsaal 9.122

Overview

This introductory session covers the course structure, expectations, and provides a first look at the central questions driving the course: What is AI in the current context? How do modern AI systems differ from human intelligence? And why is language at the center of it all?

Note: This is a half-length session focused on orientation and motivation.

Topics

  • Course logistics: 12 lectures, 3 seminar weekends, 1 exam
  • Artificial Intelligence vs. Human Intelligence
  • AI in the current context: AlphaFold, agentic AI, multimodal data
  • Key questions: What are neural networks, language models, transformers?
  • Where will AI go next?
  • Where does machine thinking come from?
