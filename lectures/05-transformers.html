05: Transformers & Self-Attention

Monday, 08 June 2026, Hörsaal 9.122

Overview

Recurrent models hit two walls: they cannot be parallelised, and the signal from far-away tokens decays as the hidden state is overwritten. Self-attention removes both walls by letting every token look at every other token directly. The lecture works through scaled dot-product attention from the query, key, value abstraction, walks one token end-to-end on a worked example, and reads off the attention matrix and the causal mask. The full transformer block follows: multi-head attention, feed-forward sublayer, residual stream.
