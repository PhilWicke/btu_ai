05 — Transformers & Self-Attention

Monday, 08 June 2026 — Hörsaal 9.122

Overview

This lecture opens the box on the transformer block. We start from the two failure modes of recurrent models — no parallelism and decaying long-range information — and motivate attention as the mechanism that replaces recurrence entirely. We then derive scaled dot-product self-attention from the query/key/value abstraction, walk through one token end-to-end on a worked example, and read off the attention matrix and the causal mask.

Topics

  • From RNNs to transformers: the sequential bottleneck and the promise of parallel attention
  • Polysemy and context: how the same token vector gets reshaped by its neighbours
  • Loss, gradient descent, and stochastic gradient descent as a one-paragraph refresher
  • Bahdanau et al. (2015): attention as soft alignment in seq2seq translation
  • Vaswani et al. (2017): “Attention Is All You Need” — recurrence removed entirely
  • Self-attention intuition: scoring relevance, softmaxing, weighted blend of values
  • Query, key, value as three learned linear projections of the same input
  • Scaled dot-product attention: the formula and why we divide by √dk
  • The attention matrix and the causal mask (upper triangle → −∞ before softmax)
  • Multi-head attention, the feed-forward sublayer, and the residual stream
  • The full transformer block, end-to-end
